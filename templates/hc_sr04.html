📏 Deep dive: HC-SR04

How a robot measures distance using sound.

The HC-SR04 uses ultrasonic sound—too high for people to hear—just like an echo in a big hall.

How the echo becomes a number

  1. TRIG: Arduino asks the sensor to make a short sound pulse.
  2. Echo: Sound bounces off an object and returns.
  3. ECHO: The sensor sends a pulse whose length tells us the travel time.
  4. Calculate: distance = time × speed of sound ÷ 2. We divide by 2 because sound travels there and back.
